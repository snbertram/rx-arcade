Refill Rocket

Your patient needs a regimen. Fly the jetpack through the pharmacy and grab the medications that fit their case. Anything harmful or interacting costs a safety heart, and so do the ECG zappers. Lose all three hearts and the run ends.

Controls: hold Space, the mouse button, or your finger to fly up. Let go to drop. P pauses.

The chart above the game shows each slot as you fill it. Only grab what this patient should take.
