Inhaler Pop

Swap neighboring tiles to line up three or more of the same brand. Each level gives you orders written as clinical prompts, like a GOLD group or a list of generics, and you have to know which brand fills them. Match 4 for a line clear, and 5 for a tile that clears every copy of an inhaler.

How to swap: drag a tile toward its neighbor, or tap one tile and then the one next to it.

Uses are simplified from GINA and GOLD. For educational purposes only. Not clinical advice.
